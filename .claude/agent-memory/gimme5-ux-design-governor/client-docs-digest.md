## 000_Presentation_Gimme5_short_ITA_survey_results.pdf (MGM survey)
- Academic survey by Gargano & Rossi on Member-Get-Member program
- Key findings:
  - Neither invitees nor inviters are very sensitive to the 5 EUR bonus
  - Bias in responses from both user categories
  - Small number of users send high number of invites (>50)
  - 90% of MGM users don't generate enough revenue to justify the bonus
- Recommendation: pay bonus only AFTER user creates a rule active for 3-6 months
- Reason for NOT inviting (non-inviters): "not knowing whom" scores highest (3.50)

## Goal-Based Investment - Executive_Summary.pdf (Gargano & Rossi)
- Goal-setting leads to 91% increase in net deposits (379 to 724 EUR/year)
- Additional 50 EUR/month in first month, stabilizes to ~10 EUR/month
- Goal TYPE doesn't matter (leisure vs durable vs generic = same effect)
- Users save MORE for shorter goals and goals < 1,000 EUR
- "Joink" feature + goal-setting = tremendous savings increase
- ~30% of goals are achieved; rest are closed/overdue/still active
- Specific goals (hobby, travel, car, casa) have 10% LOWER completion than generic "Risparmio"
- Short-term + small goals = most likely to be achieved
- MONITORING is the crucial mechanism (not the nature or deadline of the goal)
- Design implication: progress visibility matters more than goal category

## Draft_0_Recurring.pdf (Gargano & Rossi, Apr 2025)
- "Set it and Forget it: Engineering Investment Habits with FinTech"
- Data: Gimme5 app (explicitly named), Jan 2021 - Dec 2024
- 41% of users adopt automated rules; among adopters, 73% automate deposits
- Rule types: MONTH (86% of adopters, 85% of automated deposits), WEEK, DAILY, DATE, SPORT, FIT
- Monthly: ~58.5 EUR/deposit; Weekly: ~16.8; Daily: ~6.1
- Despite larger per-deposit, monthly rules yield LOWER aggregate savings (497.5 vs 516.5 weekly vs 556.3 daily)
- Daily rules discontinued after ~5 months; weekly/monthly after 8-9 months
- Event-based rules (sport, steps) = supplementary, small fraction
- Rules CAUSALLY increase savings by ~87.74 EUR/month (from baseline 26.54 without rules)
- Rules reduce trend-chasing: manual deposits correlate with past performance, automated do not
- "Return gap" (investor return vs fund return) shrinks to 0% for fully automated users (vs 1.5% for manual)
- Notifications about rules doubled adoption rate (from 15.4% to ~30%)
- Users: avg age 36, 82% male, avg 20 months active, 2.6 deposits/month, median uses 1 fund
- Fund allocation: 12% equity, 46% fixed-income, 43% balanced
- 5 data tables: Rules, Deposits&Withdrawals, Investment Vehicle, Users + FIDA fund datafeed
- Withdrawal fee: flat 1 EUR regardless of amount

## Mind_the_Gap_2024.pdf (Morningstar)
- Investors earned 6.3% vs 7.3% fund return over 10yr = 1.1% annual gap
- Gap from mistimed purchases/sales (buying high, selling low)
- Allocation funds have narrowest gap (-0.4%); sector equity widest (-2.6%)
- More volatile funds = larger gaps
- Validates GIMME5's core thesis: automation reduces timing mistakes

## Correcting Present Bias in Saving Decisions with FinTech (Gargano & Rossi, 2021)
- Goal-setting = soft, self-designed commitment device
- Causally increases saving rate (diff-in-diff with 122 beta testers)
- Additional savings: 322-658 EUR/year depending on specification
- First month: +30 EUR, stabilizes to +15 EUR/month
- Key mechanism: MONITORING (ability to track progress toward goal)
- Goal specificity doesn't increase completion rate
- Savings within app don't substitute savings outside app
