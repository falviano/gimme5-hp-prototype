# Terminology Evolution

## "Regola" -> "Routine" (and the failed alternatives)

### Timeline
1. **Pre-project**: App uses "Regola" for recurring investment rules
2. **Apr 2**: Giuseppe suggests "PAC" (Piano di Accumulo) might be clearer than "Join ricorrente". Alessandro counters: keep it simple, defer terminology
3. **Apr 8**: Old term "Join ricorrente" mentioned. No consensus
4. **Apr 15**: Giuseppe encourages changing "Regola", suggests "automatismo" or "deposito ricorrente"
5. **Apr 23**: Sarah proposes "Gimmi" (brand-linked). Andres warns against repeating "joink" mistake. Giuseppe says "routine" emerged as liked term internally
6. **Apr 29**: Wireframes start using "routine" alongside "trigger" and "sfida"
7. **May 6**: Giuseppe confirms: "Iniziamo a chiamarle routine perche piace a tutti"
8. **May 11+**: Wireframes consistently use "routine"

### Current terms [CONFIRMED May 6]
| Old term | New term | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Regola | Routine | Recurring automatic investment (monthly, weekly, etc.) |
| Joink | (deprecated) | Was the old term for a quick one-off investment |
| Patto con te stesso | Sfida | Behavioral challenge (quit smoking = save X) |
| (new) | Trigger | Event-based investment (sport results, steps, etc.) |
| (none) | Versa rapido | Quick one-off deposit (+5, +10, custom) |

### Warning: "Gimmi"
Sarah proposed it (Apr 23) as brand-linked replacement. Andres was skeptical ("same mistake as joink"). Alessandro said he's "non inventare nomi diversi per ogni singola regola." Giuseppe did not push hard. NEVER ADOPTED. Do not use.

### "Joink" context
Historical term inside the app for one-off investment. Mentioned by Marco (Apr 29) as "quello che oggi e il joink." Being deprecated in favor of "Versa rapido" / quick deposit functionality.

### "Salvadanaio"
NOT a renamed thing -- it is a NEW concept introduced by Sarah/Filippo (Apr 27-29). It is the fallback card that holds funds without a linked objective. Conceptually it is the "generic objective" or "default bucket."

### Other terminology notes
- "Obiettivo generico" = salvadanaio (same thing, different label)
- "Switch" = transfer between objectives/funds (introduced May 11 by Alessandro)
- Categories (Apr 29): Viaggio, Casa, Famiglia, Svago, Istruzione, Sport, Veicolo, Altro
- "Routine" covers ONLY ricorrenze effettive. Trigger and Sfida are separate types.
